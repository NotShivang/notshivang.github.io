### MoDocs

**Mo**bile **Doc**ument**s** — a lightweight Android app for viewing and working with documents on your phone.

There was no single lightweight app on the Play Store that supported opening PDF, DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX without showing you a bunch of ads, so I built one.

**Features:**
- **PDF** — View, search, fill forms (text, signatures, checkmarks, dates), and save filled copies
- **DOCX** — View Word documents with formatting, images, and tables
- **XLSX** — View and edit spreadsheet cells, save changes
- **PPTX** — View presentations with text, shapes, images, and backgrounds

**Requires Android 8.0 or higher.**
