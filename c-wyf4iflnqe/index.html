Private by design. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded. This page is blocked from making any network requests, and nothing is kept once you close the tab.

How to export: in WhatsApp, open the chat → contact name or ⋮ → Export chat. Without media gives a .txt with just the messages. Attach media gives a .zip, and the photos, videos, voice notes and documents show up in the chat. Either works, no need to unzip.
